Amenities

Everything you need, already on site

From Wi-Fi and a study room to backup power and filtered borehole water, every Mateo residence is set up for a full academic year.

Living

  • Wi-Fi

    Stay connected for lectures, research and assignments.

  • Furnished rooms

    Rooms come furnished, so there's no furniture to buy or transport.

  • Kitchen

    A kitchen to cook your own meals and stretch your allowance further.

  • Laundry room

    Do your washing on site, so laundry day doesn't need a trip out.

  • TV room

    A place to watch the match, catch up on series or unwind with friends.

Study and Wellness

  • Study room

    A dedicated quiet space for group work, revision and exam season.

  • Gym

    Stay active without leaving the property.

  • Play area

    Somewhere to take a proper break between study sessions.

Safety and Power

  • CCTV

    Camera surveillance to help keep residents and the property safe.

  • Access control

    Controlled entry, so only residents and authorised visitors get in.

    Power for when the grid goes down, including during load-shedding.

    Cook a hot meal even when the electricity is off.

Water

    Water from a borehole, filtered for residents' use.
